ABOUT US

A Taste of Spain in the Heart of Chicago

Nestled in the historic South Loop neighborhood, Pequeño Mercat brings the warmth and flavor of Spanish café culture to downtown Chicago. As part of the renowned Mercat a la Planxa family, we offer an authentic boutique coffee experience just steps from the iconic Blackstone Hotel.

Whether you're a morning commuter rushing to work, a Columbia College or Roosevelt University student seeking the perfect study spot, or a tourist exploring Grant Park, our cozy café provides the ideal refuge with exceptional coffee and freshly baked pastries.

We source the finest beans and ingredients, crafting each cup and pastry with the passion and precision that Spanish culinary tradition demands. Stop by for your morning espresso, grab a quick lunch, or indulge in our signature churros.
